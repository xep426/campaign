Open slot

4×2, and interactive — tap a row to complete it right here, tap the header to open the app.2 The row is the target because the mark is smaller than a thumb.

Try it

Tap a task on Today to complete it — or tap the same row on the widget, which writes back the other way. Pull into today on a campaign fills the open third slot. Promote ↑ on the end-of-day screen creates a new campaign.

Open questions — from §14

  1. Notification timing & wording. Mocked at 21:04, opening straight into the entry page.
  2. Widget interactivity. Rows toggle completion in place; the header opens the app. Costs the tick animation — RemoteViews cannot draw a path.
  3. “Next step” shape. Modelled as a single free-text line per campaign — no sub-lists.

Held back on purpose

No fourth slot, no tags, no priorities, no subtasks, no streaks, no accounts. Every screen answers one question and then gets out of the way.
